# kedversetr

kedversetr is a Turkish-language short-video channel about technology, gaming and internet culture. We publish vertical short videos on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

"kedverse publisher" is a small private tool that uploads our own videos to our own TikTok account (@kedversetr) using the TikTok Content Posting API. It is used only by the account owner and does not collect data from, or post on behalf of, any other TikTok user.
