# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

**Who we are.** "kedverse publisher" is a private tool operated by the owner of the @kedversetr account.

**What we access.** When the account owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives an access token from TikTok that lets it upload and publish the owner's own videos. Basic profile information (display name) is read only to confirm which account is connected.

**What we do not do.** We do not collect, sell or share personal data of visitors or of other TikTok users. We do not run advertising or analytics on this site.

**Storage.** The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is not shared with third parties.

**Deletion.** The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings > Security and permissions > Apps and services, which makes the stored token invalid.

**Changes.** We may update this policy by posting the new version on this page.

**Contact.** Questions can be sent through the @kedversetr account on TikTok or Instagram.
